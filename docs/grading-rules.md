# GPA and ungraded results

Checked against the linked school sources on 6 November 2026.

- **NUS:** Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory (S/U) and Completed Satisfactorily/Completed Unsatisfactorily (CS/CU) are excluded from GPA. [Registrar grading rules](https://www.nus.edu.sg/registrar/academic-information-policies/undergraduate-students/academic-structure-grading).
- **NTU:** Flexible Grading Option (FGO) uses Pass/No Record and replaced S/U from AY2024/25. Legacy S/U and courses graded only Pass/Fail are also excluded. [FGO and legacy S/U](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/sbs/admissions/undergraduate/resources-for-students/course-registration-information), [Academic Structure Handbook, section 6.2](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/docs/default-source/office-of-academic-services/academic-structure-handbook-%28undergraduate-studies%29-ay2023-24.pdf?sfvrsn=cab1b601_3).
- **SMU:** A+ carries 4.3 grade points, but cumulative GPA is capped at 4.00. Semester GPAs remain uncapped. [Undergraduate GPA scale](https://admissions.smu.edu.sg/faqs/general/what-smus-undergraduate-degree-award-system), [SMUSA undergraduate guide and grade table](https://www.smusa.sg/_files/ugd/7ab21d_0176d2851bff4009b5e4da7a6b32084b.pdf). Passed Exploratory Courses are excluded from cumulative GPA. Internships are graded Pass/Fail without GPA contribution. [SMU Core FAQ](https://cis.smu.edu.sg/programmes/faq). This does **not** mean every failed Pass/Fail course is excluded: SMU explicitly counts F for the guided external course in its [work-study FAQ](https://computing.smu.edu.sg/undergraduate/academic-experience/wsp/smu-kpmg). The calculator therefore distinguishes **F** (zero points, counts towards GPA) from **Fail (ungraded)** (excluded); users select the result appropriate to their course.
- **Poly:** The shared Poly tab offers Pass/Fail for courses with no grade points. SP calls these UP/UF, while TP spells them Pass/Fail. [SP grading table](https://www.sp.edu.sg/student-services/student-handbook/grading-system), [TP grading table](https://www.tp.edu.sg/schools-and-courses/students/schools/bus/about-bus/BUS-Grading-System.html). The option labels include SP's UP/UF codes. Do not confuse these with the similarly named Non-Graded Pass penalty grades: SP's P carries 0.5 points, TP's P carries 1.0, and NYP's NGP carries 1.0. Those numeric penalty grades are not treated as GPA-excluded results. [NYP assessment regulations](https://mynypportal.nyp.edu.sg/en/student-handbook/shb-academic-matters/shb-assessment-regulations.html).

Ungraded results contribute neither points nor credits to the GPA denominator. A regular F contributes zero points and includes its credits in that denominator. With no GPA-counted credits, GPA is unavailable rather than zero. Prior-semester credits must include only credits counted towards the entered GPA.

Receipt credit totals continue to show all listed module credits, including ungraded results; they are not a count of credits earned towards graduation. Eligibility, conversion limits, retake policies and graduation progress are outside this calculator's scope.
